# Welcome, Learning Objectives and Course Navigation

## Welcome, learning objectives, and course navigation

### Course purpose

Cloud adoption increases demand for partners who can help organizations operate AWS environments efficiently and securely. The course presents foundational operating practices with a particular focus on AWS Managed Services (AMS) and related operations tools.

### Learning objectives

By the end of the course, learners should be able to:

- Explain foundational cloud-operations ideas, including the Operational Excellence pillar in the AWS Well-Architected Framework and its role in effective operations.
- Describe why organizations move operational workloads to the cloud, common operating challenges, and how automation and scalable cloud capabilities can address them.
- Identify AWS services and features for monitoring, management, and automation that support reliable, efficient workload operations.
- Match AWS operations approaches to customer situations and business requirements.
- Find AWS Partner tools, programs, and other resources for developing a cloud-operations practice.

### Getting the most from the course

The active-learning guidance recommends keeping notes tied to current customer conversations, pausing to consider the course questions, reviewing reference materials, and planning how to use the ideas in one's role. The course is designed for about 60 minutes of focused study; learners can pause and resume, while the lesson suggests completing it in one sitting to aid retention. Resources available during the course include AWS documentation, customer success stories or case studies, and assessment questions.
