# Partner Opportunity and Resources

## AWS partner programs for Cloud Operations

The AWS Partner Network (APN) is presented as a structured growth path. Select, Advanced, and Premier tiers bring increasing benefits such as funding opportunities, technical support, and program access. Advancement is tied to AWS certifications, demonstrated customer success, and AWS expertise across the organization.

- **AWS Managed Service Provider (MSP) Program:** validates a partner's ability to provide comprehensive managed services. Qualification requires a third-party audit of operational practices, including monitoring, incident management, security, and automation. It fits partners who continually monitor, automate, and manage customer AWS environments and want an AWS-validated credential.
- **AWS Migration Acceleration Program (MAP):** supports migration-practice development with methodology guidance, tools, and potential funding to offset customer migration costs.
- **AWS Solution Provider Program:** lets partners resell AWS services while providing technical expertise, particularly when the partner manages billing relationships with end customers.

The lesson directs partners to AWS Partner Central for business-development funding. The interactive cards describe Market Development Funds (MDF) for marketing activities, Proof of Concept (POC) credits for customer demonstrations, and incentive programs tied to particular AWS services or solutions. Select programs that fit the business model and immediate customer needs, then expand participation as the practice grows.

## AWS teams that support partners

AWS Partner Solutions Architects (PSAs) can help with complex customer scenarios, architecture reviews, technical validation, customer calls, and proof-of-concept work. Maintain communication with the AWS Partner Development Manager (PDM), who can connect the partner with appropriate technical resources.

## Tools and learning resources

- **AWS Well-Architected Operational Excellence resources:** use the Operational Excellence pillar to assess operations, find gaps, and build improvement roadmaps. The framework includes checklists, design principles, and practices; use it flexibly for each customer's maturity rather than as a rigid checklist. The course suggests the Prepare section when establishing operational priorities for change management.
- **AWS Well-Architected Tool:** available in the AWS Management Console and used to review customer workloads against six pillars, identify high-risk issues, create improvement plans, and apply specialized lenses for domains such as machine learning, analytics, serverless, HPC, IoT, SAP, streaming media, gaming, hybrid networking, and financial services.
- **AWS Skill Builder and AWS Skills Center:** use focused training such as AWS Control Tower Landing Zone Architecture and AWS Observability Fundamentals for project readiness. Skills Centers add hands-on labs. For complex material or team-wide upskilling, combine self-paced courses with instructor-led learning and prioritize training linked to current project needs.
- **AWS Solutions Immersion Days:** offer intensive, hands-on practice with specific operations tools and methods such as monitoring, logging, and automation. They can accelerate proof-of-concept work or service evaluation, but do not replace comprehensive preparation for complex implementations.
- **AWS Prescriptive Guidance Library and AWS Solutions Library:** Prescriptive Guidance provides open-source templates and reference architectures for deploying third-party software and AWS-native services. Partners can adapt the default automation to a particular use case. The public Solutions Library provides vetted business and technical solutions.
- **AWS Workshops:** provide self-paced hands-on exercises for team enablement or customer discovery and proof-of-concept demonstrations. The lesson suggests workshops on Systems Manager, CloudWatch, and AWS Organizations.

Choose resources based on the immediate engagement rather than trying to use everything at once. The lesson's takeaway is that the Well-Architected Framework, training, and Immersion Days can reduce implementation complexity and improve the partner's delivery approach.
