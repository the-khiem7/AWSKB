## What the course covers

The course introduces cloud operations for AWS Partners, emphasizing AWS operational best practices, the Operational Excellence pillar of the AWS Well-Architected Framework, AWS Managed Services (AMS), and related operations tools. It aims to help partner teams understand the market context, map customer needs to AWS operational capabilities, identify suitable approaches for customer situations, and find partner resources.
