# Amazon ECS - Knowledge Badge Readiness Path

Source language: English. This learning plan contains ten trainings in the order shown by AWS Skill Builder. The page lists a total duration of 12 hours 40 minutes, English as the selected language, and a status of 0 of 10 trainings completed. The assessment is separately marked In Progress.

## Trainings

1. [Containers Learning Series for Amazon ECS Overview](01%20-%20Containers%20Learning%20Series%20for%20Amazon%20ECS%20Overview/README.md) — Digital course, Fundamental, 10 minutes. Overview of a learning series from foundational concepts to advanced techniques.
2. [ECS Introduction to Containers](02%20-%20ECS%20Introduction%20to%20Containers/README.md) — Digital course, Fundamental, 1 hour. Business and technical reasons for containers; packaging, portability, cost, namespaces, cgroups, and runtimes.
3. [Amazon ECS Fundamentals](03%20-%20Amazon%20ECS%20Fundamentals/README.md) — Digital course, Fundamental, 1 hour. Architecture, task definitions, scheduling, clusters, services, tasks, capacity providers, placement, and scaling.
4. [Deploying a Workload on Amazon ECS](04%20-%20Deploying%20a%20Workload%20on%20Amazon%20ECS/README.md) — Digital course, Intermediate, 1 hour 30 minutes. Deploying UI services and microservices with Fargate, VPC/NAT/IAM prerequisites, health checks, troubleshooting, and load balancing.
5. [Amazon ECS Security](05%20-%20Amazon%20ECS%20Security/README.md) — Digital course, Intermediate, 1 hour 30 minutes. Image scanning, Inspector, IAM, secrets, network and data protections, and runtime monitoring.
6. [Amazon ECS Networking](06%20-%20Amazon%20ECS%20Networking/README.md) — Digital course, Intermediate, 1 hour 30 minutes. VPC networking, private subnets, load balancers, and Service Connect.
7. [Amazon ECS Observability](07%20-%20Amazon%20ECS%20Observability/README.md) — Digital course, Intermediate, 1 hour 30 minutes. Logging, FireLens, monitoring, X-Ray, OpenTelemetry, logs, metrics, traces, and events.
8. [Amazon ECS Auto Scaling and Cost Optimization](08%20-%20Amazon%20ECS%20Auto%20Scaling%20and%20Cost%20Optimization/README.md) — Digital course, Intermediate, 1 hour 30 minutes. Resource limits, metrics, scaling policies, and cost optimization.
9. [Amazon ECS Deployment](09%20-%20Amazon%20ECS%20Deployment/README.md) — Digital course, Intermediate, 1 hour 30 minutes. Rolling and blue/green strategies, deployment controllers, parameters, and CodeDeploy.
10. [Amazon ECS Knowledge Badge Assessment](10%20-%20Amazon%20ECS%20Knowledge%20Badge%20Assessment/README.md) — AWS Assessment, Intermediate, 1 hour 30 minutes. The page says a score of at least 80% can earn the badge; Credly issuance is stated as 5–7 business days.
