# Introduction to the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework

## Why AWS CAF is used

AWS CAF packages AWS experience from customer engagements into a structured way to plan digital transformation. It helps an organization prioritize opportunities, check and improve readiness, and keep evolving a roadmap toward a cloud-optimized business and IT environment. Partners can use it early in a customer's journey to turn scattered adoption conversations into a complete strategy and act as a trusted technical advisor. The lesson cites a McKinsey projection that cloud adoption could create more than $1 trillion in value for Fortune 500 companies by 2030.

The framework starts from an assessment of the customer's current business and IT landscape and helps describe a target state, uncover gaps in readiness, and prepare workloads for cloud migration. It separates guidance into six perspectives. Teams use these as workstreams to identify missing skills and processes, record gaps as inputs, and assign responsibilities to groups with related functions.

## Transformation phases

| Phase | Purpose and outputs |
|---|---|
| **Envision** | Senior leadership sets the cloud vision and migration strategy, defines business objectives, desired outcomes, and success measures, identifies enabling technologies, prioritizes initiatives, and names stakeholders needed for alignment. The strategy should connect business outcomes to technology and measurable value. |
| **Align** | Stakeholders compare current readiness with the Envision vision across all six perspectives. They surface capability gaps, dependencies between groups, and stakeholder concerns, then shape targeted readiness work, change management, and an actionable plan. The lesson identifies Align as the phase that establishes the approach for the journey. |
| **Launch** | Teams execute the first cloud-adoption workstreams, migrate and deploy initial workloads, establish operating processes and support, and use production pilots to demonstrate incremental business value. Pilot results and lessons refine the approach before broader expansion. |
| **Scale** | The organization expands successful production pilots to the desired level, verifies and sustains expected business benefits, optimizes the cloud environment and operating model, strengthens governance, and pursues further innovation and transformation. |

## Partner workshop example

An **Envision workshop** brings customer senior leaders together to develop a shared readiness vision. The partner helps identify stakeholders, define success measures, connect work to business outcomes, and rank initiatives, producing a unified vision plan.

An **Alignment workshop** engages those stakeholders to organize blockers and challenges against the vision. The partner helps identify trends, turn them into actions, assign owners, and create a practical readiness plan. Once gaps are addressed, the customer and partner can identify workloads, choose suitable migration strategies, and plan the migration.
