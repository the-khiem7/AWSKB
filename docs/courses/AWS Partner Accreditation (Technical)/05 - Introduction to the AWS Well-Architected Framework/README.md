# Introduction to the AWS Well-Architected Framework

## Purpose and review approach

Infrastructure underlies every cloud application. The AWS Well-Architected Framework draws on AWS Solutions Architects' experience across industries and helps customers reason about architectural tradeoffs. It gives teams a consistent way to compare designs with AWS best practices and identify improvements for secure, reliable, efficient, cost-effective, and sustainable workloads.

A Well-Architected review is a constructive discussion of architecture decisions, not an audit. A well-designed system is more likely to support business success. The framework is useful after migration as well as during design and operation.

## Six pillars

| Pillar | Focus and examples from the lesson |
|---|---|
| **Operational Excellence** | Improve the efficiency of people and processes. Examples include controlling and automating changes, responding to events, and setting standards for daily operations. |
| **Security** | Protect data, information, and systems. Practices include least-privilege access, credential management, system protection, detection controls, and effective response to security events. |
| **Reliability** | Design highly available, resilient systems that keep business operations going through component, service, or infrastructure failures. The lesson includes foundational setup, cross-project requirements, recovery planning, and change management. |
| **Performance Efficiency** | Use AWS resources efficiently to meet business objectives. Select suitable resource types and sizes, monitor performance, and use elasticity. |
| **Cost Optimization** | Maximize value from cloud spending. Understand where money goes, select appropriate resources, and review spending over time. |
| **Sustainability** | Reduce the environmental effect of workloads. Understand impact and improve utilization so fewer resources are needed and downstream impact is reduced. |

## AWS Well-Architected Tool and remediation

The lesson contrasts the Tool with recording review answers only in a PDF or spreadsheet. The AWS Well-Architected Tool lets customers document maturity across **workloads and portfolios**, use that information to direct investment, and communicate application health. A review can surface issues and risk scores, helping partners prioritize remediation.

The example recommends tackling straightforward risks first, such as improving operating procedures or replacing more self-managed infrastructure with AWS managed services. Managed services can improve scalability, performance, and availability while helping control costs. They also free teams to spend more time on business logic and can improve the workload's design risk score.

The video frames the review as an ongoing partner engagement: applying the framework builds solution quality and best-practice expertise, supports customer outcomes, and can reveal further business needs and partner opportunities.
